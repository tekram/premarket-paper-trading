Check the math

Add up the P&L $ column in the closed table — it equals the Realized P&L on the dashboard, and it equals the final Running P&L value. Add the open table's unrealized total and you get the dashboard's Total P&L. Every setup the premarket brief produced is listed here — winners, losers, and open trades — so there is nowhere for a bad trade to hide. Formula per row: $ P&L = $10,000 × per-trade %; per-trade % is measured fill-to-exit against the verified open.
